VoiceLedger – Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: September 4, 2026

VoiceLedger allows users to permanently delete their account and associated data directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your VoiceLedger Account

  1. Open the VoiceLedger app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap your backup cloud account email address.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Enter your account password.
  7. Confirm the account deletion.

After you confirm the deletion, your VoiceLedger account will be deleted according to the app's account deletion process.

What Data Is Deleted?

When your VoiceLedger account is deleted, the account and data associated with the account that are handled by VoiceLedger for the account are deleted as part of the account deletion process.

  • VoiceLedger user account information
  • Cloud-backed-up VoiceLedger data associated with the account
  • Other account-associated data that is deleted by the app's account deletion process

Data Retention

VoiceLedger does not intentionally retain account data after a successful account deletion, except where retention is required by applicable law or where data may remain temporarily in secure backups before being automatically overwritten according to the applicable backup retention process.

If You Cannot Access the App

If you cannot access VoiceLedger and need to request account deletion, contact the VoiceLedger support team using the support contact provided on the Google Play Store listing or in the VoiceLedger privacy policy. Include the email address associated with your VoiceLedger account so that your request can be verified and processed.

Contact

For account deletion questions, please contact the VoiceLedger support team using the official support email address listed on the Google Play Store listing.
